Methodology
Project pipeline — from raw data to forecast
1
Data Generation & Loading
48 months of synthetic monthly sales (Jan 2021–Dec 2024) generated with realistic trend, seasonality, and noise. In practice, replace with real CSV/database data.
2
Cleaning & Preprocessing
Checked for missing values (none found). IQR-based outlier detection performed. Cyclic month encoding via sin/cos features to capture seasonality.
3
Train / Test Split
80/20 chronological split (38 train, 10 test). Shuffle disabled to prevent data leakage across time — critical for time-series evaluation.
4
Model Training
Two models compared: Linear Regression (baseline) and Polynomial Regression (degree 2). Both trained on time index + seasonal sin/cos features.
5
Evaluation
Metrics: MAE, RMSE, R² on test set. Linear Regression achieved R²=0.60; Polynomial achieved 0.26 — simpler model wins here (bias-variance tradeoff).
6
12-Month Forecast
Polynomial model extrapolated 12 months ahead (Jan–Dec 2025). Projections show continued upward trend with mild seasonality, peaking in Dec 2025 at ~333.6 units.
